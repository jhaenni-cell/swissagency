The problem

Great content that nobody sees is wasted money.

You record a one-hour podcast. You publish it once. A few hundred people watch it. Then it sits on YouTube forever.

Meanwhile, your competitors post every day. They aren’t smarter or more interesting than you. They’re just more visible. On social media, the brand people see most often is the one they trust and buy from.

Short-form is where attention lives today. Most brands know it. Few have the time, editing skills or consistency to win there.

Our approach

Long-form is your asset.
Short-form is your distribution.

One hour of content holds 20 to 40 moments worth sharing. We find them, cut them for retention and publish them relentlessly. More clips means more chances to go viral. And more data on what your audience actually wants.

01

Volume wins

The algorithm rewards consistency. We publish every day, so you get dozens of chances each month to reach new people, not just one.

02

The first 2 seconds

Every clip opens with a hook built to stop the scroll: a bold claim, a tension point or a question your audience needs answered.

03

Repurpose the winners

When a clip performs, we don’t move on. We re-cut it with new hooks, formats and framings to squeeze every view out of it.

04

Distribution at scale

Your accounts plus a network of dedicated clip pages. That’s more surface area and more ways for people to find you.

05

Data, not guesswork

We track retention, watch time and shares on every clip. Then we do more of what works and cut what doesn’t.

06

Views that convert

Every clip is designed to send people somewhere: your profile, your podcast, your product. Attention only matters when it turns into business.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does a clipping agency do?

We take your long-form content and turn the strongest moments into short vertical videos with hooks, captions and pacing built for retention. Then we publish and distribute them on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, every day.

I don’t have much content. Can we still work together?

Yes. A single recorded conversation or a handful of phone videos per month is enough to start. We’ll also help you plan simple formats that give us more material to work with.

How much of my time does this take?

Almost none. After a kickoff call, all we need is access to your footage. We handle editing, captions, posting and reporting.

How quickly will I see results?

Your first clips go live within 72 hours. Short-form rewards consistency, so within the first weeks we have clear data on which topics and formats your audience responds to. From there, we scale them.

Who owns the content and the accounts?

You do. Everything published on your accounts is yours, and so is every clip we create for you.

What does it cost?

Every partnership is tailored to your goals, content volume and platforms. Book a call, and we’ll put together a proposal that fits your brand.
