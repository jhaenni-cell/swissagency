The approach

Long-form is the asset.
Clips are the distribution.

Most brands post one polished video a week and hope the algorithm is kind. We do the opposite: find the strongest moments in what you already have, cut them for retention, and publish relentlessly. Volume creates data. Data shows what works. Then we do more of what works.

01

Volume beats perfection

One great clip can change a month. You find it by shipping daily — not by polishing a single post for weeks.

02

The hook is everything

The first two seconds decide whether anyone sees the rest. Every clip opens with tension, a bold claim or a question worth staying for.

03

Repurpose, don’t repeat

A winning clip gets new life with a fresh hook, captions, framing or format — so the same moment reaches new audiences on every platform.

04

Distribution at scale

Your main accounts plus a network of dedicated clip pages. More surface area, more shots on goal, more people discovering you.

05

Data decides

We track watch time, retention and shares per clip, double down on the formats that hit, and drop what doesn’t.

06

Built for growth, not likes

Views are the start. Every clip is designed to send people back to you — your profile, your podcast, your product.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a clipping agency?

We take long-form content — podcasts, streams, interviews, webinars — and turn the best moments into short vertical videos. Then we publish and distribute them across TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts so more people discover you.

Do I need to film new content?

No. Most clients already have hours of footage. If you don’t, a single recorded conversation or a few phone videos per month is enough to get started.

How fast will I see results?

First clips go live within 72 hours of receiving footage. Short-form rewards consistency, so we usually see clear patterns in what performs within the first 30 days.

Who owns the content and accounts?

You do. Everything posted on your accounts is yours. Clip pages we run on your behalf can be handed over if you leave.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All plans are monthly. We’d rather keep you because the results speak for themselves.
